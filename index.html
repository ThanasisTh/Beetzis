Data sources & scoring criteria

Where the data comes from:

  • OpenStreetMap (via the Overpass API) — beaches, existing campsites/hotels, access tracks, protected areas/nature reserves, towns/cities, and forest/wood cover. Queried live for whatever's in view each time you click Scan.
  • Curated enforcement hotspots — a small, manually researched list of areas with documented camping enforcement (fines, patrols, arrests), each with a source link.
  • Enforcement reports — Google News RSS searched weekly for Greek/English camping-fine keywords, filtered to hits that both name a specific Greek region and actually mention camping.

How a beach is scored (0–100):

  • Isolation (up to 50 pts) — distance to the nearest campsite/hotel.
  • Access (up to 30 pts) — distance to the nearest track/path. The popup for each beach also shows that path's type (dirt track, footpath, minor road, etc.), surface, tracktype grade if it's a track, its name if mapped, and whether it's tagged private access — informational only, it doesn't change the score.
  • Shade (up to 20 pts) — proximity to a forest/wood patch, but only full credit if there's also enough forest area nearby, not just a thin sliver of mapped trees. Patches tagged access=private or that look enclosed by mapped fencing are excluded entirely — though untagged, unfenced private land can still slip through, since OSM only knows what's been mapped.
  • ≥70 → Prime, 40–69 → Possible, <40 → Unlikely.
  • Forced to Unlikely regardless of score if 2+ businesses cluster within 1 km, or a real town/city (not a village) is within 1 km.
  • Forced to Restricted if inside a mapped protected area — this overrides everything else.
  • Forced to Enforcement hotspot if within a known active-enforcement area, unless already Restricted.

Read first

Wild camping is illegal nationwide under Law 5170/2025 (in force since Jan 2025, refined by Law 5209/2025 from July 2025): on-the-spot fines are €300 per person/vehicle, with older Law 4055/2012 allowing arrest and fines up to €3,000 for flagrant offenses. Enforcement varies sharply by area — see the hotspot markers and reports below. This tool surfaces quiet, undeveloped spots for scouting; it is not legal advice, and it doesn't check land ownership or fire bans. Verify locally before camping anywhere.
