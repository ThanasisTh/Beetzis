Read first

Wild camping (pitching a tent outside a licensed site) is technically illegal in Greece under Law 392/1976, though enforcement varies a lot by area. This tool surfaces quiet, undeveloped spots for scouting — it is not legal advice, and it doesn't check land ownership, turtle-nesting beaches, or fire bans. Verify locally before camping anywhere.
